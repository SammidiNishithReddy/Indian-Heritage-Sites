UNESCO

UNESCO, the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, plays a vital role in fostering peace and development through education, science, culture, and communication.

World Heritage Convention

It is an international treaty that aims to protect and preserve cultural and natural heritage sites of outstanding universal value. It combines nature conservation and cultural preservation, emphasizing the importance of maintaining a balance between people and their environment.

Indian Heritage

India’s cultural heritage is a vibrant tapestry woven from diverse threads of languages, religions, dance, music, architecture, and customs. It spans millennia, influenced by ancient civilizations, native faiths, and foreign religions.
